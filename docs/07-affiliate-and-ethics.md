# Affiliate monetization and ethics

Educational content can use referral programs, but trust is more valuable than a commission.

## Required disclosure

Place a clear notice near every referral link:

> This is an affiliate or referral link. I may receive credits or a commission if you register or purchase through it. This does not increase your price unless the provider explicitly states otherwise.

## Recommended funnel

```text
Educational video or post
        ↓
Independent tutorial and comparison
        ↓
Clearly labelled provider links
        ↓
Optional referral conversion
```

The tutorial should remain useful when the reader never clicks a referral link.

## Do not promise

- guaranteed savings,
- guaranteed income,
- guaranteed access to scarce GPUs,
- guaranteed training results,
- permanent fixed prices,
- that one provider is best for every workload.

Provider eligibility, payout methods, country availability, advertising rules, attribution windows, and commission rates change. Read current terms before running paid advertising.

Official RunPod references:

- https://www.runpod.io/referral-and-affiliate-program
- https://docs.runpod.io/accounts-billing/referrals

Never invent a partner link or claim approval before acceptance.

Suggested placeholders:

```text
RUNPOD_REFERRAL_URL=YOUR_RUNPOD_REFERRAL_URL
GOOGLE_CLOUD_PARTNER_URL=YOUR_APPROVED_GOOGLE_CLOUD_PARTNER_URL
```
